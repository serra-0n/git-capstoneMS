INTRODUCTION 
Project Context 
The resort industry continues to apply digital technologies to increase operational 
efficiency, reservation management, and customer service. However, many resorts still 
face operational challenges due to manual reservation processing, paper records, 
disconnected communication channels, and conventional transaction processing. Such 
operational problems could cause delays in confirming reservations, booking conflicts, 
inaccurate record keeping, slow document verification and difficulties in managing guest 
transactions and resort operations. 
Based on initial observations and data collection made from selected private and public 
resorts in National Capital Region, many resort establishments still use manual operational 
procedures and messaging applications in managing reservations, accommodation 
availability, payment monitoring, and guest -related transactions. As these operational 
activities are often managed manually or through separate digital platforms, resort owners 
may face challenges in keeping organized records, tracking reservations, and  processing 
client documents efficiently.  These might cause an impact on operations like service, 
coordination, productivity and overall customer experience with the resort establishment.

The Philippine Statistics Authority (2024) reported that the accommodation and food 
service activities sector in the Philippines continues to increase, and become part of the 
tourism and hospitality industry of the country. As hospitality operations are becoming 
more customer -centered and transaction -oriented, the demand for centralized and 
accessible digital systems is also increasing. However, several resort esta blishments still 
do not have integrated web-based platforms that can manage reservations, accommodation 
monitoring, billing transactions, and document processing within a single operational 
environment. 
To address these operational concerns, the proponents propose a Web -Based Resort 
Management System as a Software as a Service (SaaS) with Optical Character Recognition 
(OCR) for selected private and public resorts in NCR. The proposed study is to provide a 
centralized SaaS based platform where the resort owners can register and manage the resort 
operations and the clients can browse the resorts, process the reservations, uplo ad the 
documents and monitor the booking transactions through a web-based environment. With 
Software as a Service (SaaS), a number of resorts can access the platform with centralized 
web technologies, while keeping separate resort information and operational records within 
the system.

Background of the Study 
Due to the growth of the hospitality and tourism industry, the resort establishments have 
adopted the use of digital technologies to improve their operational efficiency, 
management of reservations, and customer service. As resort operations become more 
transactional, many establishments are expected to handle large volumes of reservations, 
guest information, billing records, and operational documents efficiently. However, many 
resorts still use manual reservation processing, paper records, spreadsheets and messaging 
applications to manage their day -to-day operations. These conventional practices could 
lead to delay in reservation confirmation, clashes in booking, erroneous transaction records, 
sluggish document verification and trouble in keeping systematic operational information. 
According to the Philippine Statistics Authority (2024) the accommodation and food 
service industry will continue to grow as the Philippine tourism and hospitality related 
services continue to develop.  With the increasing demand of customers for online 
reservation systems and digital hospitality services, it is also expected that resort 
establishments will improve operational accessibility and transaction management by 
using modern web-based technologies. Even with the digital tools available, many resort 
businesses face difficulties in managing reservations, confirming payments, handling 
accommodations, and processing documents through a unified operational platform.

The importance of web -based management systems and Software as a Service (SaaS) 
platform has grown in terms of enhancing accessibility, centralized management and 
operational efficiency in hospitality businesses. SaaS based platforms offer web -based 
services to multiple establishments through centralized online systems, removing the need 
for separate installations and expensive infrastructure. The centralization of access can 
facilitate the operation of resort owners in managing reservations, accommodations, client 
transaction, and operational records while ensuring system accessibility through internet -
connected devices. 
In addition to reservation and transaction management, document verification and 
information extraction are still key operational activities in resort establishments. 
Documents that resort usually handle during reservation and transaction processes are valid 
IDs, proof of payment, reservation forms, and guest -related records. However, manual 
checking and encoding of information from uploaded documents could be a time -
consuming and risky process for incomplete and inaccurate data records. The use of Optical 
Character Recognition (OCR) technology helps in more efficient ways to extract text-based 
information from uploaded files and digitize operational documents for easier verification 
and record management.

standard internet browsers on computers, laptops, tablets and mobile devices that are 
connected to the internet. The centralized SaaS structure allows resort owners to register 
and operate resorts on one online platform but still have separate operational records and 
resort information. Guests can view information about resorts and accommodations 
available without registering. Registered clients can make reservations, upload operational 
documents and track reservation transactions through the system.

Scope and Limitations 
Scope 
This study is about the development of a Web-Based Resort Management System as a SaaS 
with OCR for the chosen private and public resorts in NCR. This system is intended to 
consolidate all the major resort operations in one web -based platform such as reservation 
management, accommodation tracking, billing transactions, document verification through 
OCR, staff coordination, reporting and operational monitoring. The system is designed to 
assist in reducing manual operational procedures, improving organizational efficiency and 
providing centralized access to resort information to authorized users. 
The proposed system will be designed as a web -based application that can be accessed 
through computers, laptops, tablets and mobile devices which are connected to the internet 
via common internet browsers. The system will implement centralized database 
management and role-based access control to improve operational organization and secure 
management of resort information. Through the SaaS -based system offered, the resort 
establishment can manage the operational activities independently based on the operational 
needs and service requirements of the business.

Limitations 
• System Coverage 
The proposed system is intended only for private and public resorts in NCR. The 
study focuses only on reservation management, accommodation monitoring, billing 
transactions, OCR-based document verification, staff management, reporting, and 
operational monitoring within resort establishments. Other hospitality sectors such 
as hotels, restaurants, travel agencies, and transportation services are outside the 
scope of the study. 
• Third-Party Booking Integration 
The proposed system will not include direct integration with external booking 
platforms, online travel agencies, or third -party reservation systems. Reservations 
originating from external platforms must be manually entered into the system by 
authorized personnel. 
• Digital Payment Verification 
The proposed system supports manual digital payment verification through GCash, 
Maya, and bank transfer transactions. Clients may submit proof of payment or 
transaction reference numbers within the system, while authorized resort personnel 
manually verify submitted payments before updating reservation or billing status. 
However, direct banking integrations, automated real -time financial processing, 
and advanced third -party payment gateway services are outside the scope of the 
study. 
===PAGE===
   
 
 
31 
STI College Muñoz-EDSA 
• OCR Document Processing Accuracy 
The OCR functionality of the proposed system depends on the readability and 
quality of uploaded documents. Blurred images, damaged files, handwritten 
information, poor lighting conditions, and low -resolution uploads may affect the 
accuracy of text extraction and document verification results within the system. 
• Advanced Analytics and Artificial Intelligence 
The reporting and analytics module will generate operational and financial 
summaries based on available system data. However, advanced predictive analytics, 
artificial intelligence forecasting, automated business intelligence features, and 
machine learning-based recommendations are outside the scope of the study. 
• Mobile Application Development 
The study focuses only on web -based implementation accessible through internet 
browsers. A separate mobile application version for Android or iOS devices will 
not be developed during the current stage of the study. 
• Internet Connectivity Dependency 
The proposed SaaS -based platform requires stable internet connectivity to access 
system functionalities, process reservations, upload operational documents, and 
manage resort operations. System accessibility and operational performance may 
be affected during internet interruptions or connectivity issues.
